Logo

One logo, four colorways

Pick the colorway that reads best on your background. Never redraw, stretch, or recolor it.

Do

  • Use blue on white, cream, or yellow.
  • Use white on blue, coral, red, or photos.
  • Leave clear space equal to the height of the red dot on every side.
  • Keep it at least 120 px wide on screen, 1 inch in print.

Don't

  • Stretch, squash, or rotate it.
  • Recolor it outside the four colorways.
  • Add shadows, outlines, or effects.
  • Place it on a busy or low-contrast background.
  • Combine it with another logo to make a new mark.
