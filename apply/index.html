Who we're looking for

Our listener is early. Pre-revenue, maybe solo, no cushion. We look for guests who can give that person one of five things:

A peer story. You're in it right now, and hearing you say it's hard makes someone else feel less crazy.

Literacy. How a system or service actually works, so they aren't blindsided later.

Future planning. How to think about a decision they'll face at a later stage.

What not to do. The expensive mistake your field watches beginners make over and over.

A core skill. Beginner-level sales, management, operations or finance. Something they can go do.
