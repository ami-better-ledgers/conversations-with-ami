Meet your host

Who is Ami Morgenstern?

I learned business from the inside: office and account management, then bookkeeping, then Financial Controller roles. I saw where the numbers held together and where they didn't.

Now I run companies that help entrepreneurs:

  • Better Ledgers
    Bookkeeping and back-office support for growing businesses. Founded July 2022.
  • Dash Staff
    Offshore staffing, so you can build a real team from a global talent pool. Co-founded January 2024.
  • qewlabs
    We design how a business should run, then build the custom software to run it. Launched November 2026.

A bigger audience helps those companies, and I'll always say so.

I wasn't good at podcasting at first. I got better by staying on what's valuable and doing the prep. I'm still building. So are you.
