Our Companies: Better Ledgers, Dash Staff and qewlabs

I run three companies, and a bigger audience helps all three. Here's what each one does, in plain words.

What are Ami's companies?

A bigger audience helps these companies, and I'll always say so. When one comes up in an episode, an email or a post, I say so the first time.

  • Better Ledgers

    Bookkeeping and back-office support for growing businesses.

  • qewlabs

How do the companies connect to the show?

The show came from the same instinct as the companies: giving early-stage entrepreneurs the things worth knowing before they need them. Guests are chosen for what they can teach, not as clients. And when a company comes up, I say so the first time.

Which one is right for me?

  • I can't see where my business stands → Better Ledgers
  • I need to hire, and I can't afford a local team → Dash Staff
  • My business runs on spreadsheets and I can't see it clearly → qewlabs
