What is Dash Staff?

Offshore staffing that lets entrepreneurs build a real team from a global talent pool. Co-founded January 2024.

A bigger audience helps this company, and I'll always say so. Here's the whole deal.

What roles can Dash Staff fill?

Back-office roles such as virtual assistants, customer service, administrative support, data entry and bookkeeping. Their services page lists the kinds of staff they place.

How does hiring offshore work?

Dash Staff explains the steps on its own site, so the details stay accurate if they change.

How is Dash Staff connected to the show?

Dash Staff exists because of a problem we ran into ourselves: we couldn't find great bookkeepers through the offshore staffing agencies we tried, so we figured we'd have to do it ourselves. Guests aren't Dash Staff clients by default; they're chosen for what they can teach.
